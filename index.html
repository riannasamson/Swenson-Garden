Swenson Community Garden (Rianna Samson)

Swenson Community Garden was established in 1999 on this property owned by Heritage Presbyterian Church. Dr. Ed Swenson, the garden’s founder, wanted to strengthen community resilience by increasing access to sustainable, regional sources of food.

As part of Sustainable Solano’s Permaculture Design Course, my team of four is planning a project to enhance and expand the garden using permaculture principles to keep Dr. Swenson’s mission alive.
